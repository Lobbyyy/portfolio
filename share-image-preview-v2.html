Design Direction

Stoicism — Restraint. Not everything needs to be filled. Let negative space do work.

Wisdom — The words are the hero. Typography hierarchy matters. Title dominates.

Minimalism — Every element earns its place. No decoration for decoration's sake.

Consistency vs. Platform — The sidebar creates brand recognition across formats. But Stories might benefit from a bolder, different approach that still feels "on-brand."
