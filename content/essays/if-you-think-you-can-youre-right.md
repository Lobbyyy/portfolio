It got me thinking, why does belief matter so much?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vsR3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1b6cc216-5f8e-4b5d-9821-d671b5d30f51_1331x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vsR3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1b6cc216-5f8e-4b5d-9821-d671b5d30f51_1331x176.png)It matters because belief determines your destiny.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3tBx!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafc6e21e-b586-4337-9fe1-17a829b0ce5b_1331x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3tBx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafc6e21e-b586-4337-9fe1-17a829b0ce5b_1331x176.png)Your belief determines your attitude.

Your attitude determines your actions.

Your actions determine your habits.

Your habits determine your character.

Your character determines your destiny.

**The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do**

-Steve Jobs
