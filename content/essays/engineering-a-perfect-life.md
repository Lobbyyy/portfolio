I’ve pondered this for quite a while.

After all, who doesn’t want a perfect life?

I propose an answer

or

an approach to finding the answer.

It combines two principles from different domains.

Economics and Physics.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ieqp!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbc80ba11-a142-4635-a0f2-30fbb900a9b3_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ieqp!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbc80ba11-a142-4635-a0f2-30fbb900a9b3_2856x328.png)**A perfect life is a life that is perfect *****for you*****.**

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MbcW!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6017cbde-1cc8-45f3-9430-68f8a9890ab7_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MbcW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6017cbde-1cc8-45f3-9430-68f8a9890ab7_2856x328.png)It has to be perfect *for you*.

Not your parents,

not your grandparents,

not your extended family,

not your partner,

not your crush,

not your friends,

not your kids,

not your manager,

not your employer,

and most definitely not social media.

It has to be perfect *for you*.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N-jS!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F42693aea-f15e-4797-99d5-191c8140ff41_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N-jS!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F42693aea-f15e-4797-99d5-191c8140ff41_2856x328.png)In economics, you learn about the allocation of scarce resources.

Well, there is nothing more scarce than your life.

When I refer to life,

I’m referring to the time spent animated between life and death.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2kXB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3a34ab8-4ddd-4540-86f1-9b81ddbf11b1_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2kXB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3a34ab8-4ddd-4540-86f1-9b81ddbf11b1_2856x328.png)In Physics, you’re taught first principles thinking.

I didn’t study physics but learned this principle from [my board member](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/half-my-board-is-dead?r=r7j0y), Elon Musk.

This line of thinking involves breaking down complex ideas and problems into their fundamental parts.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!sBAB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0b4ac63d-687d-427d-a15e-02c0b6a1c8d6_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!sBAB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0b4ac63d-687d-427d-a15e-02c0b6a1c8d6_2856x328.png)Combining economics and physics offers us a unique perspective,

a perspective grounded in logical soundness and practicality.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PPMX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a3f10b1-7014-405b-a779-9f7924932c5d_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PPMX!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a3f10b1-7014-405b-a779-9f7924932c5d_2856x328.png)If life is the time spent animated between your first breath and your last.

Then, life is the time spent alive.

Therefore,

life is a collection of years,

years are a collection of months,

months are a collection of weeks,

weeks are a collection of days,

days are a collection of hours,

hours are a collection of minutes,

minutes are a collection of seconds.

So,

life is a collection of seconds.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4yGy!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5e375080-7726-4f66-9a41-bff4244aa66e_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4yGy!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5e375080-7726-4f66-9a41-bff4244aa66e_2856x328.png)**If you want a perfect life, perfect every second.**

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9tLt!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a814a36-daaf-41d1-a506-f94e23624827_2856x328.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9tLt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a814a36-daaf-41d1-a506-f94e23624827_2856x328.png)If every second is perfect, then every minute is perfect.

If every minute is perfect, then every hour is perfect.

If every hour is perfect, then every day is perfect.

If every day is perfect, then every week is perfect.

If every week is perfect, then every month is perfect.

If every month is perfect, then every year is perfect,

If every year is perfect,

then you’ve lived a perfect life.
