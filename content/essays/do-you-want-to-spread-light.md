[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pBxL!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F32e7be9e-5cbb-4373-8fb6-185c1a542a73_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pBxL!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F32e7be9e-5cbb-4373-8fb6-185c1a542a73_878x127.png)The Sun bathes our solar system in endless light,

a celestial dance of energy and warmth.

Every moment, it transforms darkness into possibility,

turning empty space into a canvas for life.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!LF_p!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F99d67452-ba0e-4403-9550-b16c39ce8a0b_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!LF_p!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F99d67452-ba0e-4403-9550-b16c39ce8a0b_878x127.png)The Sun is our source of light, warmth and life.

It is a special star.

It is our star.

We appreciate the Sun for all it does for us,

but we often overlook the Moon.

The Moon also provides light, warmth and life,

but it does so in a different way.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_jr0!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4b90041f-1eb5-4c4f-9306-9c8008247a04_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_jr0!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4b90041f-1eb5-4c4f-9306-9c8008247a04_878x127.png)The Sun creates light.

The Moon reflects it,

but both achieve the same outcome.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rBzq!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb55f3791-04d2-418d-95a1-a3878b673bb0_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rBzq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb55f3791-04d2-418d-95a1-a3878b673bb0_878x127.png)As a human, I want to spread light in the universe.

I want to spread positivity, compassion and joy.

I want to be the Sun,

but in the beginning, I didn't know how,

so I first learnt to be the Moon instead.


[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t9Em!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F157e9b90-5087-4155-97b8-cd7dbf7d25fa_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t9Em!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F157e9b90-5087-4155-97b8-cd7dbf7d25fa_878x127.png)In the Sun's core, atoms are being joined together in the process of nuclear fusion.

This process creates a lot of heat and light.

So when you look up at the sky during the day,

that ball of light is just a grand nuclear reaction.

A reaction where nuclear energy transforms into light and warmth.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kC_x!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F87597cd9-ede0-48fc-949b-c1fd8b643dbb_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kC_x!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F87597cd9-ede0-48fc-949b-c1fd8b643dbb_878x127.png)The first law of thermodynamics tells us that energy in the universe is never lost or gained.

Energy is always conserved; it merely transforms from one state to another.

Giving us the illusion of loss when really it's just change.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hgSO!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcb43ff31-1009-4c21-a8a8-6dff39502c20_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hgSO!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcb43ff31-1009-4c21-a8a8-6dff39502c20_878x127.png)When you're sad,

or depressed,

or angry,

or vengeful,

or ashamed,

or insecure,

you have a particular type of energy.

When you're happy,

or joyous,

or grateful,

or proud,

or in love,

or secure,

you have a different kind of energy.

Moving from one end of this energy spectrum to another is a transformational process.

Similar to how the Sun transforms nuclear energy into heat and light,

or how wind turbines transform the wind's kinetic energy into electrical energy.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kfg5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5a202f60-0b96-4ead-9bfa-b57bdf74bfca_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kfg5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5a202f60-0b96-4ead-9bfa-b57bdf74bfca_878x127.png)In the long run, you should learn to transform your energy.

You should learn to be the Sun; produce your own light and radiate it across the universe.

It's the only sustainable way to be happy because your happiness becomes an independent variable.

A variable over which you have complete control and autonomy.

Your happiness won't depend on anyone or anything or any one event.

It becomes an ever-flowing internal stream, just like the light from the Sun.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3EFm!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe9dff629-fadb-4f6c-beaf-866ccc7af61d_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3EFm!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe9dff629-fadb-4f6c-beaf-866ccc7af61d_878x127.png)If you're struggling with creating your own light,

don't worry — it's a process.

But also,

realise that you don't have to create your own light.

You can simply reflect it, just like the Moon.

You can reflect the positivity of others around you.

In doing so, you also spread light.

It may not be to the same degree as the source of light,

but even the faintest reflection can pierce through the darkness.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!OA6G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4c1b7f2b-eeb2-4312-a8a2-1dfd234f3efc_878x127.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!OA6G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4c1b7f2b-eeb2-4312-a8a2-1dfd234f3efc_878x127.png)Keep reflecting light and warmth; keep mirroring the energy around you.

Let the light of others bounce off your surface,

spreading the change you seek.

Keep doing this, and one day,

perhaps without you noticing,

you'll find your own inner Sun beginning to glow.
