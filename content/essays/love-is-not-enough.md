peace is not enough for a relationship between nations.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZFUo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7e9d7c9f-401d-4f5e-b2b0-c6c5d4642d5f_2856x398.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZFUo!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7e9d7c9f-401d-4f5e-b2b0-c6c5d4642d5f_2856x398.png)If you want a relationship to work in the short run, love helps.

It helps a lot.

It helps if you love your partner

and your partner loves you.

If you want a relationship to work in the long run,

you need a lot more than love.

You need understanding.

You need respect.

You need alignment.

You need the relationship to ‘make sense’.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!jcSJ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ae0efae-efcc-4206-8686-6ac447a3c41b_2856x398.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!jcSJ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ae0efae-efcc-4206-8686-6ac447a3c41b_2856x398.png)Similarly,

if we want a peaceful relationship between nations,

it is simply not enough to strive towards peace.

Peace in itself should not be the goal.

The goal should be prosperity.

A world where everyone is winning.

A world where people and nations choose not to play zero-sum games.
