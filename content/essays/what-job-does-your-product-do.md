We went to a bar near London Bridge called Nine Lives,

definitely recommend it - it was a vibe.

The Negronis were decent, too.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KPGB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa91fcc54-ed87-4e61-84b9-df76358948ee_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KPGB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa91fcc54-ed87-4e61-84b9-df76358948ee_2856x396.png)Unfortunately, the next day, I woke up with a cold.

And it has progressively got worse.

Chest pains,

migraines,

fever,

night sweats,

sore throat.

Typical flu symptoms.

The most annoying symptom is a sore throat because I love eating and swallowing food has been painful.

So, last night, I did something unusual.

Unusual for me, at least.

I went to *Afters*, a dessert chain and ordered a milkshake.

I did this because I wanted to soothe my throat.

I imagined it as a cold ice pack for my throat,

a remedy for my inflamed tonsils.

As I sat there waiting for my milkshake to be prepared,

I recalled something I learnt in the *Disruptive Strategy* course at Harvard Business School -

The Job to be Done (JTBD) framework.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GrAo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8f6c50a0-003b-485a-91aa-f0209ce75562_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GrAo!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8f6c50a0-003b-485a-91aa-f0209ce75562_2856x396.png)Learning about the JTBD framework created a massive shift in my perspective.

I suddenly looked at the world differently. 

It has affected every product decision I have made as a startup founder.

The framework says people do not buy products; they hire them.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5Tzn!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F581627fe-bc3b-437f-a6d1-326d2cab0e50_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5Tzn!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F581627fe-bc3b-437f-a6d1-326d2cab0e50_2856x396.png)The JTBD framework was developed by Tony Ulwick and popularised by Clayton Christensen.

Clayton was also my lecturer for the Harvard Course.

He is a great teacher.


[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Zmqf!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F097e993f-6a79-415f-b367-525f8bce1a1b_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Zmqf!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F097e993f-6a79-415f-b367-525f8bce1a1b_2856x396.png)The easiest way to understand this framework is to know how it came about.

Some years ago, McDonalds wanted to increase the sales of its milkshakes, and they tried a bunch of different tactics.

They expanded the breadth of flavours,

made it more chocolaty,

made it cheaper,

etc.,

but to no avail.

So, they hired Clayton as a consultant to help them solve this problem.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!e3bB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F78cacad3-a0e6-4a4a-ad13-38030b4503b5_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!e3bB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F78cacad3-a0e6-4a4a-ad13-38030b4503b5_2856x396.png)Clayton did what consultants do best - research.

He started off by observing how the milkshakes were being sold and consumed.

Patterns started to emerge.

He noticed that 40% of the milkshake sales occurred before 8:30am.

He also noticed that most purchasers were drivers on the way to work.

Many of them followed the same consumption pattern:

These drivers were typically alone;

they did not buy anything else;

and they consumed their shakes in their cars.

He delved deeper by asking those purchasers some qualitative questions.

The most important one being

**"Tell me about a time when you were in the same situation but didn't buy a milkshake. What did you buy instead?"**

The answers surprised him and laid the foundation for the JTBD framework.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!m4Lj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F65ecee26-0f64-40a2-af1f-c7f97c5854a3_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!m4Lj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F65ecee26-0f64-40a2-af1f-c7f97c5854a3_2856x396.png)He learned that when these customers didn't purchase a milkshake,

they bought other goods.

They bought a bagel.

Other times, they bought a banana,

even doughnuts on occasion.

Inquiring further, he discovered why these customers were actually drawn to the milkshake.

Bagels are dry and a bit messy to eat, especially on the commute to work.

Bananas were consumed a bit too quickly, leaving commuters bored,

and the satisfaction brought on by a doughnut was short-lived as by 10am, hunger would strike again.

In opposition to this, a milkshake solved all of these problems simultaneously.

It was easy to consume with one hand and left no mess.

It was viscous and took about 20 minutes to consume through a thin straw, so commuters were not quickly bored on their journey,

and it was filling enough that they were not hungry by 10am.

It became clear what *job* this milkshake was doing.

It was being hired to solve the tedious commute job.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tcYZ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb687de9f-7de5-4980-9e2d-f5f2bf38c99d_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tcYZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb687de9f-7de5-4980-9e2d-f5f2bf38c99d_2856x396.png)These findings were significant because they shifted the question.

**The question transformed from:** How can we boost the sales of our milkshakes?

To - How can we solve the boring commute?

By focusing on the JTBD,

they figured out how to do the job better.

They make the milkshake thicker, so it lasts longer on the commute.

They added tiny chunks of fruit, adding some unpredictability to a monotonous commute,

and they changed the positioning of the milkshake machine to make it easier for drivers to pick it up on the go.

These changes resulted in an increase in sales of milkshakes by 7x.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MW97!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9f426224-377d-4f13-9524-d68d74d33802_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MW97!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9f426224-377d-4f13-9524-d68d74d33802_2856x396.png)Today, I feel better.

My sinuses have cleared up.

I am not full of phlegm,

and each cough is not scrunching up my lungs. 

I am definitely on the mend,

and I can not help thinking if it was because I hired the milkshake last night.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wEOF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a080802-56aa-4689-ba79-a949b01d9f98_2856x396.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wEOF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a080802-56aa-4689-ba79-a949b01d9f98_2856x396.png)**Takeaways:**

Be curious - are you asking the right questions?

Understand your product and the job it does.

Pistachio, Ferrero Roche and Kinder Bueno are fire combos for a milkshake.
