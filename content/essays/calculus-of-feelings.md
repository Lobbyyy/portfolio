[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)What if we approached emotions with a first-principles thinking?

Breaking down complex feelings into their most fundamental components.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)There are some core feelings. 

The atomic units of emotion. 

These fundamental elements make up all the complex feelings we experience,

like atoms that make up matter.

Just as chemistry shows us how simple elements create infinite complexity, 

emotions work the same way.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)Take carbon. 

The foundation of all life. 

Arrange carbon atoms one way and you get soft graphite. 

Arrange them differently and you get diamonds. 

Same element, completely different properties.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)But combine different elements entirely and you unlock new possibilities. 

Two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom create water. 

One sodium atom with one chlorine atom creates salt. 

One carbon atom with two oxygen atoms creates carbon dioxide.

Feelings operate in the same way. 

Our emotional experiences aren't random. 

They're compounds, built from fundamental elements.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)Examples of basic elements?

Happiness, sadness, fear, anger, surprise, trust, and anticipation.

Everything else you feel is likely a derivative. 

A combination of these core components.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)Let me show you what I mean:

Combine happiness and sadness, and you get nostalgia. 

Combine fear and anticipation and you get excitement. 

Combine anger and sadness, and you get resentment. 

Combine trust and fear, and you get vulnerability. 

Combine happiness and surprise, and you get delight.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)The complex emotions that confuse you? 

They're just compounds you haven't broken down yet.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)That feeling of nostalgia when you're looking at old photos?

It's happiness for those beautiful moments,

and sadness that those moments are gone. 

The happiness comes from reliving the joy. 

The sadness comes from knowing you can't go back.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)That feeling of betrayal when someone you trusted lies to you?

It's anger at being deceived. 

Sadness that the relationship isn't what you thought,

and the surprise that they were capable of this. 

The anger comes from being wronged. 

The sadness comes from losing something you valued. 

The surprise comes from your trust being shattered.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)That excitement after a first date that goes perfectly? 

It's happiness from the connection you felt.

Anticipation for what might come next,

and hope that this could be something special. 

The happiness comes from genuine enjoyment. 

The anticipation comes from the possibility. 

The hope comes from imagining a future together.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mJ27!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fafa74289-5dac-48e6-b491-cf4cb0b63c3c_2140x332.png)Here's why this matters:

Feelings can be incredibly complex, 

but when you understand the components of that feeling, 

you understand the feeling itself. 

And when you understand the feeling, 

you understand yourself better in that moment.

You stop being confused by your emotions. 

You start recognising the patterns. 

You begin to see that even your most complex feelings have simple, understandable roots.
