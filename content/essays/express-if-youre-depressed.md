[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wzIn!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F99ce697c-37b7-4e17-b5bd-592bf9327db8_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wzIn!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F99ce697c-37b7-4e17-b5bd-592bf9327db8_2856x370.png)When I think of depression,

I think of the Sun.

The Sun looks stable; it shows up every day without complaints.

It illuminates our path;

it gives life;

and without it, the world would be a cold place.

Think *Snowpiercer* - but colder, way colder.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VS2L!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F38313fcc-576b-4c06-861c-b7a063e1caa9_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VS2L!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F38313fcc-576b-4c06-861c-b7a063e1caa9_2856x370.png)In reality,

the Sun is in a constant battle for survival.

Just like us.

It only exists because the competing forces holding it together are in balance.

These forces are in opposition to each other,

and for now,

these forces are in equilibrium.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!---9!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89121313-43b2-45b7-875b-2f051cd6ceb0_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!---9!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89121313-43b2-45b7-875b-2f051cd6ceb0_2856x370.png)There is one external force pushing in,

and one internal force pushing out.

The force pushing in is gravity,

and the force pushing out is thermal pressure.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!g0uw!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1aeef48f-9ab3-4331-9367-faaa618c21f7_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!g0uw!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1aeef48f-9ab3-4331-9367-faaa618c21f7_2856x370.png)If the forces of gravity pushing in is greater than the force of thermal pressure pushing out,

The Sun shrinks.

It grows smaller and smaller

and smaller and smaller,

until finally, it implodes.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-9lC!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4c4bba49-dfae-4dc6-bbd3-1b96a3ff4485_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-9lC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4c4bba49-dfae-4dc6-bbd3-1b96a3ff4485_2856x370.png)If the forces of gravity pushing in is weaker than the force of thermal pressure pushing out,

the Sun grows.

It grows bigger and bigger

until it becomes so big that it can no longer sustain itself.

It runs out of energy, is overwhelmed by the forces of gravity, and it implodes.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mcGy!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feaa8ca80-1583-45fa-8c56-1f8d82bbf697_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mcGy!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feaa8ca80-1583-45fa-8c56-1f8d82bbf697_2856x370.png)In both scenarios, the Sun ceases to exist.

The Sun can only exist in a state of equilibrium.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!M_qt!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F49517320-c1ee-466b-b6fb-a80ab05db8a9_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!M_qt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F49517320-c1ee-466b-b6fb-a80ab05db8a9_2856x370.png)I know the feeling of depression really well.

[My story](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/my-story) has been riddled with it.

It feels like gravity.

It feels like the world is pushing in on you.

An external force outside of yourself.

It just keeps pushing,

and pushing,

and pushing.

You resist, but for how long?

Eventually, you start to shrink.

You grow smaller and smaller every day.

The change is only noticeable with time.

One day, you wake up and do not recognise yourself anymore.

You have shrunk so much that you become a shell of yourself,

of who you truly are.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UO3H!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3bddf37d-bcfb-4413-a539-a088824d14d3_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UO3H!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3bddf37d-bcfb-4413-a539-a088824d14d3_2856x370.png)If this is you,

I am sorry.

I feel your pain.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Xivp!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81de17ac-3656-41c5-8f00-27ba9b3be140_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Xivp!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81de17ac-3656-41c5-8f00-27ba9b3be140_2856x370.png)Gravity will always exist as an external force pushing you in.

It exists for us all.

It is a constant.

Some of us feel it more,

some of us feel it less,

but it’s omnipresent.

We all feel it because it is a natural force.

It exists in nature.

[Nothing unnatural exists in nature.](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/do-you-ever-question-your-mortality?r=r7j0y)

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hCPK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff79d5b01-05c5-495f-8fe2-d9f3a560f5c0_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hCPK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff79d5b01-05c5-495f-8fe2-d9f3a560f5c0_2856x370.png)The only way to oppose this force of gravity is to cultivate an equal and opposite force.

Just like how the Sun has thermal pressure,

you need an internal force pushing out.

**A force of human expression.**

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YUn_!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdb4aab90-7857-463b-aa7a-b3ed495eff24_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YUn_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdb4aab90-7857-463b-aa7a-b3ed495eff24_2856x370.png)Expression can take many forms,

art,

music,

pottery,

learning,

writing,

programming,

reading,

exercising,

podcasting,

cooking,

meeting new people,

travelling.

The list goes on.

Your imagination is the only limit.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!B1KP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdca5a5ea-1f46-4640-b87f-453eaaf69d17_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!B1KP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdca5a5ea-1f46-4640-b87f-453eaaf69d17_2856x370.png)For example,

I express myself by breathing.

I express myself by exercising.

I express myself by playing sports.

I express myself by journaling.

I express myself by telling my friends how I really feel.

I express myself by building tech products that should exist.

I express myself by putting others first.

I express myself by helping wherever I can.

I express myself by telling jokes and bringing joy.

I express myself by singing and dancing.

I express myself by connecting with people.

I express myself by connecting ideas.

I express myself by connecting ideas and people.

I express myself by helping others achieve their goals.

I express myself by achieving my goals.

I express myself by practising gratitude.

I express myself by loving unconditionally.

I express myself by believing in people.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GHv5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc0c59fb5-76da-4d29-9ae4-3ea96fd4cdb7_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GHv5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc0c59fb5-76da-4d29-9ae4-3ea96fd4cdb7_2856x370.png)Everyone is different.

Everyone expresses differently.

Find ways to express yourself and cultivate your internal force.

This internal force may be tiny at the start,

but trust me,

it will grow,

and grow,

and grow.

Cultivate it.

In the beginning, it may not be sufficient to oppose gravity,

but give it time.

Be patient.

Experiment.

Find new ways to express,

and keep expressing.

Express because you want to express,

not because it is a hot trend,

or because it is sexy,

or because everyone else is doing it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vgdM!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F34307099-9534-4c7c-a0c1-57e63651603e_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vgdM!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F34307099-9534-4c7c-a0c1-57e63651603e_2856x370.png)Your internal force will compound over time,

making it easier and easier to express.

Just like gravity caused you to shrink over time,

your internal force will cause you to grow over time.

Keep pushing out until you reach equilibrium.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VBig!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa22bf638-a1a5-49c4-9488-bd529cb67472_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VBig!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa22bf638-a1a5-49c4-9488-bd529cb67472_2856x370.png)How do you know if you have reached equilibrium?

When gravity no longer consumes you.

You’re not trying to constantly fight it and get rid of it.

You respect its existence.

You recognise its purpose,

and you learn to co-exist with it.

A perfect balance.

Ying and Yang.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bmfQ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4bb7632c-2af0-45d8-8a45-ba709829d718_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bmfQ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4bb7632c-2af0-45d8-8a45-ba709829d718_2856x370.png)[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!s2AU!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F44680675-d7ce-4b2b-818a-6d1df5eaa8df_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!s2AU!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F44680675-d7ce-4b2b-818a-6d1df5eaa8df_2856x370.png)The Sun has been in equilibrium for 4.6 billion years,

and lucky for us,

it will be in this state for another 5 billion years.

If it wasn’t in equilibrium,

you wouldn’t be reading this,

and I wouldn’t be writing this,

and we wouldn’t exist.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!uMKN!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fec01603d-0c59-45ec-bc92-f48cf0c4eeed_2856x370.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!uMKN!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fec01603d-0c59-45ec-bc92-f48cf0c4eeed_2856x370.png)From afar, it looks like the Sun is ‘doing great’,

but a closer inspection reveals a deeper story of survival.

I guess what I’m trying to say is check up on your friends,

check up on your family,

check up on the people you care about,

and be kind to everyone you meet,

because you never know the forces they are battling.
