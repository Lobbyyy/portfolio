It's the second time I've gone this week, and it feels great.

Last week, I was down with the flu, which prevented me from going.

I love going to the gym to throw some weight around.

It's therapeutic.

I'm fortunate because my gym also has a sauna and steam room.

I've been utilising this frequently, and it hits the spot.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!J1eu!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe334282c-cb93-48dc-b39b-cb98d3a8a316_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!J1eu!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe334282c-cb93-48dc-b39b-cb98d3a8a316_2936x360.png)I have built a community in the gym.

I see a few individuals often,

and I've got to know them better over the last few months.

It's been really lovely getting to know them and their stories.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PRn5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7e95d0d4-10a1-4ef4-a682-19179573611d_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PRn5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7e95d0d4-10a1-4ef4-a682-19179573611d_2936x360.png)During the sauna session yesterday, I spoke to one of these individuals.

He had recently come back from a trip to Poland.

I asked him about his trip, and that conversation stuck with me.

He mentioned seeing family and friends and celebrating All Saint's Day there.

He’d recently just come back from a trip to Poland.

He mentioned going to see family and friends, and celebrating All Saint’s Day there.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_o9h!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4dfcf9af-2207-4540-bc39-9fc66f94bcab_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_o9h!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4dfcf9af-2207-4540-bc39-9fc66f94bcab_2936x360.png)In Poland, there is no concept of Halloween.

It is not a holiday that is celebrated.

Instead, they celebrate All Saint's Day.

It is a public holiday that happens on November 1st every year.

All stores and schools are closed, and families often go to church.

This tradition has been going on for over a thousand years.

What is the purpose of this tradition?

To celebrate and remember family members who have passed away.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gPHF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffac9d348-09a6-4375-885c-467436dcc6dc_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!gPHF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ffac9d348-09a6-4375-885c-467436dcc6dc_2936x360.png)Families buy candles,

they buy flowers,

and they visit their family members' graves.

The candles help the ones that have passed find their way in the dark,

while the flowers represent the origins of the family.

By night, graves around Poland are covered in flowers and light.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZhpC!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fee17e313-75c7-4677-b785-52a08b7ecc54_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZhpC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fee17e313-75c7-4677-b785-52a08b7ecc54_2936x360.png)As he was describing the event,

I couldn't help but imagine a cemetery full of life;

full of colour;

full of fragrance;

and full of light.

It must be a beautiful sight to behold, a sight that glows like a warm ember in the cold mist of night.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!oXmM!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4190fe81-eb24-4b3d-a884-6686a4c516e9_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!oXmM!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4190fe81-eb24-4b3d-a884-6686a4c516e9_2936x360.png)It's natural to be scared of cemeteries.

I definitely was when I was younger.

After all, cemeteries are synonymous with horror films and tragic events.

As I have got older, I have questioned life, 

and I've questioned death.

My appreciation for cemeteries has grown significantly.

I've realised that cemeteries aren't a scary place.

In fact, It is one of the most loved places in the universe.

If you have lost someone,

a loved one,

a friend,

a partner,

someone who means something to you,

then the cemetery is the only place you can visit to see them again.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!D9q3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6658cf49-1105-4fc1-afb2-781625440fbd_2936x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!D9q3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6658cf49-1105-4fc1-afb2-781625440fbd_2936x360.png)All Saint's Day is nourishment for the soul.

Remembering the dead gives the present moment potency.

It is a reminder that life is short;

love transcends time;

and family will always be family.
