Most of it is worthless.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)Here's how you determine between worthless and valuable advice:

# Level 1: Competence Check

Can this person actually help me get where I want to go?

Have they walked this path before?

Do they understand the specific challenges I'm facing?

---

## Level 2: Intention Check  

Why are they giving me this advice?

Are they invested in my success?

Or are they projecting their own fears and limitations onto me?

---

# Level 3: Relevance Check

Does this apply to my specific situation?

Or are they giving generic advice that sounds wise but means nothing?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)Most advice fails at Level 1.

Some passes Level 1 but fails Level 2.

Very little makes it through all three filters.

When advice passes all three levels, pay attention.

When it doesn't, smile, nod, and move on.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c-Q4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52ab0702-91ec-45ca-8fb8-a2e257932846_1220x212.png)Your life is not a democracy.

You don't need everyone's opinion.

You need the right opinion at the right time.

Choose your advisors and friends as carefully as you choose your direction.

Both determine where you end up.
