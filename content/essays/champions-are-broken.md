[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N2H-!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6c638d80-258d-49a9-9909-4940c5fc6edd_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N2H-!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6c638d80-258d-49a9-9909-4940c5fc6edd_2938x360.png)**"Champions are broken.**

**People look at champions and try to find something.**

**Something that the champion has that other people don't.**

**It's not that at all.**

**It's that champions lack something that everyone else has ... an off button.**

**They just don’t stop."**

-Alex Hormozi

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8zEZ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1d7a5be9-1a42-4373-9c61-9db4ae8bfebe_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8zEZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1d7a5be9-1a42-4373-9c61-9db4ae8bfebe_2938x360.png)If you want to be a champion,

don't stop.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FQKP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F555add1a-6d6b-452e-b448-c487e533982b_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FQKP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F555add1a-6d6b-452e-b448-c487e533982b_2938x360.png)I used to hate that I couldn't switch off.

I used to hate that I was obsessed with my goals.

I used to hate that my dreams kept me up at night.

I used to hate that I was constantly on the grind.

I used to hate that I was proud but never satisfied.

I used to hate that I didn't know when to quit.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!TrT2!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9639fb8f-39d5-4eab-9caf-14b397c5b71c_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!TrT2!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9639fb8f-39d5-4eab-9caf-14b397c5b71c_2938x360.png)I thought there was something wrong with me.

I thought I was broken.

I thought I was damaged goods.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wiqh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9cdccde7-0471-40f0-bc6a-2855e8bfbb6f_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!wiqh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9cdccde7-0471-40f0-bc6a-2855e8bfbb6f_2938x360.png)This year, I accepted who I am.

Instead of hating this side of me,

and trying to sever it like an infected limb,

I unified it with my identity.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N9Ha!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb3a5a523-00f8-40d8-9d5f-7e7763aec4a7_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!N9Ha!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb3a5a523-00f8-40d8-9d5f-7e7763aec4a7_2938x360.png)I stopped trying to control it.

I stopped trying to neuter it.

I stopped trying to change it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!88t5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F005c014a-bd70-442c-9360-90a7a21b1796_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!88t5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F005c014a-bd70-442c-9360-90a7a21b1796_2938x360.png)I let it engulf me.

I stopped fighting the current.

I accepted it as a feature, not a bug.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-XKV!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0e9ffe1e-dd76-473d-8444-2743b3ffa3fe_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-XKV!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0e9ffe1e-dd76-473d-8444-2743b3ffa3fe_2938x360.png)I know I am a champion.

My life situation doesn't reflect this right now,

but it's only a matter of time before [my visions](/essays/the-vision), goals, and dreams become a reality. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_DDo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa0a54d80-0361-49e4-9656-a3a68a8bdbc5_2938x360.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_DDo!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa0a54d80-0361-49e4-9656-a3a68a8bdbc5_2938x360.png)How do I know this?

[Because I believe](/essays/where-does-your-belief-come-from).

Because I refuse to stop.
