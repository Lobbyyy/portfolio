[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9lFt!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a18fab8-f31b-4e42-bff0-222459bbc982_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9lFt!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6a18fab8-f31b-4e42-bff0-222459bbc982_2858x372.png)I am sitting in a coffee shop in Milan.

I just ordered a croissant and a flat white.

I am a bit hungover from the party last night.

The party was on a rooftop.

It was a great vibe.

What stood out were the people.

I met a few entrepreneurs just like myself,

and we got talking.

I keep getting asked if I am here for business or pleasure.

To me, business is pleasure.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hTIv!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F83d8c7c7-6acd-49b2-93a9-31fed4d7ade6_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!hTIv!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F83d8c7c7-6acd-49b2-93a9-31fed4d7ade6_2858x372.png)It is my first week in Milan,

I moved into my Airbnb a couple days ago.

I was in Venice the week before,

and Florence before that,

and Palermo before that,

and Bologna before that,

and Pisa before that.

Before I was in Italy, I was in Germany.

I have never been to Germany before.

I enjoyed Berlin and Frankfurt,

mainly because I had friends there,

and hanging with them was a vibe.

Cologne, Munich and Stuttgart were excellent.

I did much work,

but its gravity was not strong enough to keep me there.

I don't know where to go next.

I might stay in Milan for a bit,

this city does speak to me.

I am meeting some VCs and family offices this week,

courtesy of the entrepreneurs I met last night,

so it may extend my stay here for a bit.

Maybe Amsterdam is next? or Switzerland?

Let's see.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MTQz!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436591e5-a0ec-47aa-9a66-c0b7eff25bbb_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MTQz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436591e5-a0ec-47aa-9a66-c0b7eff25bbb_2858x372.png)I take the first sip of coffee,

Italian coffee hits differently.

I open my laptop and get to work.

My day starts off by removing any blockers for my team.

I respond to emails, Slack messages, and WhatsApp messages.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bzR9!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe5eaea85-3fcc-47bb-8705-278c3bc07812_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bzR9!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe5eaea85-3fcc-47bb-8705-278c3bc07812_2858x372.png)I am generating £10,000+ monthly recurring revenue (MRR) across 3 applications.

Most of the revenue (£7k) is coming from one app

The other two combine for £4k.

My current focus is scaling all the apps to £10k MRR each.

The £7k app is progressing on the suitable vector,

and it is only a matter of time.

The other two apps have some sideways action, requiring more attention.

It is no issue, though,

I love a good challenge.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Z5In!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdbd5275e-2ddc-4e7d-adc7-3ee55b2ac9a9_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Z5In!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdbd5275e-2ddc-4e7d-adc7-3ee55b2ac9a9_2858x372.png)As I bite into the buttery croissant,

and the flakes melt into my mouth,

I am reminded of the croissants I used to have in Gail's bakery.

Back when the vision was just a vision.

I remember the feeling of being broke.

I remember the feeling of starving for this vision.

I remember the moments I lost my mind for this vision.

I remember my sacrifices to make the vision a reality,

which keeps me grounded.

I know the value of money,

and I do not spend it on luxury goods.

I have no need to impress others around me.

I only care about living a life without borders,

wearing some nice threads,

being able to make decisions for myself,

treating my family and friends,

sending out positive vibrations into the world,

and reinvesting retained profits to make my apps better.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AT80!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9b419e7d-a268-4be5-bc4f-f054d8f293d7_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AT80!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9b419e7d-a268-4be5-bc4f-f054d8f293d7_2858x372.png)I take my salary as a dividend, and my travel, lodging, food, and expenses are linked to my startup.

I am my startup.

Even with all this travel, you would be surprised at my burn rate.

It is all about financial engineering.

Plus, now I have an accountant to handle everything for me.

All in all, my burn rate is meager.

The most significant expense across my apps is talent,

and I do not mind paying for this.

In fact, I am happy to pay more, but I only work with missionaries.

People who care about the mission.

They are working for equity,

and they care about the business.

They do not want more money.

They do what they love,

and are invested in the startup's success.

I am blessed to be working with these individuals.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3ARB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff839babb-a633-4188-a7ba-9869aeeb91ea_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3ARB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff839babb-a633-4188-a7ba-9869aeeb91ea_2858x372.png)I care about my startups.

I care for it like my own children.

I want them to live.

I want them to survive.

I want them to grow.

I want them to thrive.

Therefore, to relieve financial pressures, I have diversified my income sources.

I am generating revenue from YouTube, SubStack, Instagram

and my Full Stack Founder course.

In total, these sources bring me £6k MRR,

but getting there has been no joke.

It has taken 2 years to build up to this,

and my only strategy to get there has been to share with the world.

Share my knowledge,

share my journey,

share my self.

And the reason I share is because I care.

I care about people and the world.

Most of this revenue comes from sponsorships.

I have built quite a following on YouTube, Instagram and Substack,

and companies want access to my audience.

I do not see them as my audience.

We are all friends,

and friends help each other.

My sponsors include Miro, Loom, Anthropic, COS, Nike, Alo, Notion and Brilliant, to name a few.

It is a dream to work with them,

primarily because I use their product and believe in their mission.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MW8M!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff37c7b1a-938f-4cac-8ba9-4b1782b3d6c0_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MW8M!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff37c7b1a-938f-4cac-8ba9-4b1782b3d6c0_2858x372.png)I recently launched the Full Stack Founder course.

This is a culmination of all of what I have learned over the last 9 years.

It took a bit of time to put together.

All the startup disciplines, all the core knowledge, and how to apply it.

It is the *Minimum Viable Knowledge* required to build a successful startup.

I hope it helps other entrepreneurs out.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0phs!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5b47ce73-9ec4-442b-9a8a-9d73c3576851_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0phs!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5b47ce73-9ec4-442b-9a8a-9d73c3576851_2858x372.png)I often think about what my life would be like if I still lived in Nepal.

Where would I end up? What would I do?

There is no way to tell.

It is a counterfactual,

a parallel universe that exists only in the quantum realm.

I do know one thing, though.

If I was in Nepal right now,

I would have no idea about how to start a tech business.

On average, a 29-year-old living in London or San Francisco is closer to startup land.

And is more likely to start a startup than a 29-year-old living in Kathmandu.

I imagine the difference in degrees of separation.

The separation is caused by many factors.

The most important ones are access to funding,

access to talent,

and access to technology.

However, knowledge is the great equaliser.

Knowledge is leverage.

With this course, I envision taking a sledgehammer

and smashing all barriers to entry.

I imagine breaking the walls between the East and the West.

Prosperity should not be localised to the West

and should not only belong to a specific group.

We live in a world of abundance,

and the whole world should benefit from prosperity.

In economic terms, I am moving the market towards perfect information,

reinvesting back into my home country,

a reverse brain drain, so to speak.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!RGSX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F71b13486-a444-456d-b214-823df4bdafe8_2858x372.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!RGSX!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F71b13486-a444-456d-b214-823df4bdafe8_2858x372.png)Now, 

someone growing up in Nepal and the UK will have equal access to knowledge.

What they do with that knowledge

and how they leverage it 

is up to them.

In the long run, desire is the only differentiator.
