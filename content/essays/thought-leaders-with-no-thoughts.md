Someone who leads with thought.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!TFZ3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2deb6889-c80a-4425-975a-bbe73389d446_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!TFZ3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2deb6889-c80a-4425-975a-bbe73389d446_2858x368.png)I have seen the insides of many tech companies, both big and small,

and it is alarming to see how many 'thought leaders' have no thought.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3UQT!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F556e8e19-0e34-46ca-af56-a5ad3038e7ac_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3UQT!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F556e8e19-0e34-46ca-af56-a5ad3038e7ac_2858x368.png)Thought leaders without thoughts are just leaders.

Where do these so-called 'leaders' lead you?

Nowhere new.

Nowhere that is worth going.

A farmer leads sheep to the slaughter.

Just cause you are being led does not mean you have to follow.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0kLF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F719120a8-c35b-428c-9529-3db3d5ea36cd_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0kLF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F719120a8-c35b-428c-9529-3db3d5ea36cd_2858x368.png)Maybe they have thoughts,

but they are too scared to share them.

Maybe they have thoughts,

but they aren't their own thoughts.

Maybe they have thoughts,

but they still need to be fully formed.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YBjC!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F511061dc-b56d-4d79-8a4e-4c403bcdeb82_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YBjC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F511061dc-b56d-4d79-8a4e-4c403bcdeb82_2858x368.png)Genuine thought leadership is not recycling viral or trending posts.

It's not regurgitating the thoughts of others;

or repeating company talking points;

or posting what your PR team wrote;

or playing it safe with popular opinions.

It most definitely is not the thoughts generated by artificial intelligence.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7rAF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff289f309-83a6-4910-b33c-26039b8f7669_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7rAF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff289f309-83a6-4910-b33c-26039b8f7669_2858x368.png)Genuine thought leadership is having original perspectives;

it is taking intellectual risks;

it is speaking uncomfortable truths;

it is standing up for something real;

it's leading with your thoughts.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Nbw5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9e28acfe-7a65-41e7-8e3b-a6b8d83f184b_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Nbw5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9e28acfe-7a65-41e7-8e3b-a6b8d83f184b_2858x368.png)To be a thought leader, you have to lead with thoughts.

You have to put your thoughts out there,

and they must be *your* thoughts.

Not your marketing teams;

not the PR teams;

not your line managers;

not your AIs.

They have to be *your* thoughts.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PqON!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3fdffc5-03de-46a3-96eb-9959711533ef_2858x368.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PqON!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff3fdffc5-03de-46a3-96eb-9959711533ef_2858x368.png)Maybe your thoughts are right,

maybe your thoughts are wrong.

How do you know?

In Schrödinger's cat experiment, the cat is both dead and alive when the box is closed.

Similarly, your thoughts are both right and wrong if they stay in your head.

Open the box, put your thoughts out there and find out.
