[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!qi-r!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0dc13e0b-2bd9-4f4a-96ac-d7c0200239ee_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!qi-r!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0dc13e0b-2bd9-4f4a-96ac-d7c0200239ee_2848x414.png)My name is Lobsang Lama.

I was born in Kathmandu, Nepal.

My parents and I moved to the UK when I was 9.

Being the first generation and coming from Nepal, we didn't have much.

My parents always worked hard to put food on the table and a roof over my head,

I appreciate this now, but I always wanted more as a kid.

My parents sacrificed a lot to move to the UK,

so I've always wanted to make the most of the opportunities here.

I worked hard in school and got good grades.

I played table tennis, competed on the national circuit, and was ranked top 20 in the UK.

I attended one of the top universities in the world, and I felt like my life was on the right trajectory.

I also fell in love for the first time, which was terrific.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7ov8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdb41ad77-b46c-4088-9f86-a724061e6228_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7ov8!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdb41ad77-b46c-4088-9f86-a724061e6228_2848x414.png)Then, life slowly started to crumble around me.

The relationship broke up, and I didn't know how to deal with it,

It affected my grades, and I slowly lost confidence in myself.

I was depressed for 2 years.

I struggled to get out of bed, and even though I looked happy externally,

internally, I was unhappy and insecure.

As someone who had always excelled in everything I'd put my mind to,

I didn't know how to deal with failing at a relationship,

and failing to get the grades I wanted,

and failing to get the job I wanted.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!adXz!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F66c73d38-1165-4171-9d80-a79b6a82f5cd_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!adXz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F66c73d38-1165-4171-9d80-a79b6a82f5cd_2848x414.png)Fortunately, I got a job working in a big tech company, which brought more colour to my life.

I was making decent money, I made new friends, and I started to experience a different side of life.

I was enjoying life again,

but even then, something didn't feel right.

This voice in my head told me that I was settling.

In my heart, I knew I could do more.

I knew I had so much more potential in me.

So, I set a new goal:

To start a startup.

I knew it would be challenging, but that's precisely why I wanted to do it:

To prove to myself that I could.

Also, it would be cool to be my own boss and build something that never existed.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!C0dy!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5427d173-3902-46ed-8716-bbd18fe54a18_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!C0dy!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5427d173-3902-46ed-8716-bbd18fe54a18_2848x414.png)I had no idea how to do this, though;

my parents are both teachers, and I don't come from money.

The environment I grew up in wasn't entrepreneurial, and I didn't know any Entrepreneurs.

So I focused on learning.

I took startup courses, read business books, and watched countless YouTube videos.

I was doing my full-time job and spent my spare time up-skilling.

While others were taking their holiday days to go on holiday,

I was using my PTO days to learn my craft.

I even built up enough confidence to start my first company - PlanIt.

A travel startup designed to help friends book group holidays more efficiently.

But Covid happened,

and it killed my business.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!I8J4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff242b3e9-d9d9-444d-8ff8-7b4576948d04_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!I8J4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff242b3e9-d9d9-444d-8ff8-7b4576948d04_2848x414.png)I found myself in a familiar place:

dejected,

depressed,

and disappointed.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4GGD!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F74cb2d45-429f-4e19-aefc-aa4cd7197154_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4GGD!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F74cb2d45-429f-4e19-aefc-aa4cd7197154_2848x414.png)I changed jobs because I knew I couldn't work in that company any longer,

It was killing my soul.

I went to Strategy Consulting, and although this wasn't my goal,

it felt like I was a little closer to it.

It was a boutique firm, and we were working with several startups.

Thinking about strategy

and executing it brought me joy.

I loved talking to Founders and helping them bring their products to market.

It tickled my intellectual curiosity, and I enjoyed the responsibility that came with it.

However, by my second year there, I hit a glass ceiling.

We weren't growing at the rate that satisfied me, so I left.

I didn't know what to do next,

but I knew one thing.

My next stop would be working on my goals or helping other startup founders achieve theirs.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mYoW!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4d49fe3f-ab9e-415e-bd29-c7f79043079e_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!mYoW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4d49fe3f-ab9e-415e-bd29-c7f79043079e_2848x414.png)At that point, I didn't have any startup ideas.

I'd also moved out of my parent's home, so I needed something that would pay the bills,

so I started to look for jobs in Venture Capital.

I wanted to get into this industry so bad,

so bad in fact, that I cold-emailed most of the VCs in the UK with my CV.

I just wanted a shot and wanted someone to take a bet on me,

and in return, I would give them my all.

I got a few interviews and even some late-stage interviews, but they didn't work out.

They probably looked at my grades and discounted me without consideration for someone with a more 'appropriate' background.

I was fighting for scraps in the bottom of the barrel.

I had some savings to rely on,

but I got caught up in the irrational exuberance of the crypto markets, and most of it got wiped out when the markets crashed.

I found myself in an all too familiar place:

Dejected, depressed and disappointed.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ol6j!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F339c93f4-c3a2-4153-9894-5e6170502467_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ol6j!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F339c93f4-c3a2-4153-9894-5e6170502467_2848x414.png)But this time, it wasn't just in my mind.

I was so stressed that my physical health started to deteriorate.

I woke up one day with a bald patch on the crown of my head and even some patches on my chin.

I got diagnosed with Alopecia, and it turned my world upside down.

As a 25-year-old with no family history of Alopecia, I started to question my life and my life decisions.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Flum!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8ef7ab23-6504-4da4-a3df-9afb09fa3a83_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Flum!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8ef7ab23-6504-4da4-a3df-9afb09fa3a83_2848x414.png)I knew that I had to make some radical changes in my life.

I was sick and tired of being sick and tired.

I was sick of being depressed,

of thinking negatively,

and of constantly beating myself up for not being good enough.

I needed to rewire my brain and my body.

I needed to let go of all my previous patterns and habits and form a new approach,

a new way of thinking.

I needed to change how I saw myself, and I did precisely this.

I went to therapy.

I discovered stoicism.

I embraced yoga,

incorporated breathing exercises,

and started to be more active again.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FRGu!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2195f94a-d948-47ef-b824-4549cd65b63e_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FRGu!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2195f94a-d948-47ef-b824-4549cd65b63e_2848x414.png)**Subscribe for access to practical business frameworks and startup insights!** 🚀

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-12E!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff8a8fb41-6853-48c2-8bc4-0d9ccb147073_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-12E!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff8a8fb41-6853-48c2-8bc4-0d9ccb147073_2848x414.png)A few months in, the changes became apparent.

My self-awareness deepened,

my thinking grew more rational,

and I found a new sense of balance.

Most importantly, I began to recognise my emotional predispositions not as a weakness to overcome but as a strength to embrace.

I even got a new job at R3 in the Venture Development team.

It wasn't Venture Capital, but at least it had 'Venture' in it.

It was a step in the right direction.

I started as an intern and was barely making enough to pay the rent and cover living expenses, but it was something.

I was getting paid a third of what I was earning before, but I was at least three times happier than I was before.

The culture at the company was great, and it was the first time I felt like a part of a team.

I talked to founders all the time, and I enjoyed helping them.

I saw myself in them and wanted to help them as much as possible.

I helped them refine their pitch decks,

helped them fundraise,

helped them develop their product,

organised demo days to showcase their apps,

provided introductions to key stakeholders in the R3 network,

and unblocked their blockers.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bCz4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52623349-e755-4fad-9ba1-522810348df6_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bCz4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52623349-e755-4fad-9ba1-522810348df6_2848x414.png)After a few months in the role, I got a full-time position and was ecstatic.

My health started to recover, and the Alopecia slowly went away.

As someone who's always been focused on self-growth, I revel in change and evolution.

However, I found myself not wanting to change.

I liked where I was and who I was,

but if there is one constant in life — it's change.

It's inevitable.

The only variable is the rate of change.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5bXy!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F772fcd67-0dc3-4cf8-881c-8af05c607b54_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5bXy!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F772fcd67-0dc3-4cf8-881c-8af05c607b54_2848x414.png)After a year and a half, I was made redundant.

The company had mismanaged its burn rate, and the adoption of its product was slow.

There were multiple rounds of layoffs, and everyone in the firm was questioning, 'Who was next?'

I don't remember what round I was in, and it doesn't even matter.

All that mattered was that I loved working there and could no longer work there.

It was a difficult time, and I nearly cried during my last meeting with my team.

They didn't know what I had gone through and how much that place meant to me.

It was the medium through which I rebuilt myself, and not having that any more was scary.

I thought it might be the start of a spiral of deprivation again, and I didn't know where the spiral would end.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!779c!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcf67bed8-4d6b-4a48-a73c-7f0d9a748b08_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!779c!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcf67bed8-4d6b-4a48-a73c-7f0d9a748b08_2848x414.png)But this setback felt different.

I didn't experience the familiar cycle of dejection, depression, and disappointment.

All of the rewiring of my brain and body had worked.

This time, I had a solid foundation to process and deal with this unfortunate event.

After getting Alopecia, I promised myself that I'd never compromise on my health.

I promised never to let stress, anxiety, or fear affect my health again.

I knew it was just a job,

and no matter how much I loved it,

I'd rather lose a job than lose my sanity or my health.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!osyB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2e806dca-b449-4040-a15a-8791ee118414_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!osyB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2e806dca-b449-4040-a15a-8791ee118414_2848x414.png)The redundancy was unfortunate, but it came at a fortunate time.

I'd booked a trip to Kuala Lumpur, and this change in scenery was exactly what I needed.

It was one of the best trips of my life, and I'm not just saying that because of the food and the experiences I shared with my closest friends.

During the trip, I was actively applying for jobs, and during this search, I came across something that would change my life.

I noticed that Antler, one of Europe's most active seed-stage investors, was looking for aspiring entrepreneurs for their next cohort.

I jumped on a few calls with entrepreneurs who had gone through the program, and they referred me.

I did my first-round interview on Monday, and it went well.

I was confident in myself and my abilities.

I also had a whole list of experiences building startups and helping other startup founders.

I did my second round interview on Friday at Dubai airport while waiting for my connecting flight back to London.

I remember scouting the entire airport for plug points and the strongest wifi signal.

Funnily enough, as soon as I jumped on the call with the Partner interviewing me, she asked me to move to a quieter location.

I hesitantly found another spot.

I'm not religious, but at that moment, I requested God to provide me with good internet for at least 30 minutes.

I got my connecting flight back to Heathrow, and as soon as the flight reached cruising altitude, I got on the Wifi and checked my inbox.

I got in!

I was over the moon.

The hard part was trying to contain my excitement on the plane.

I was shrieking with excitement, and I most definitely looked crazy to anyone sitting behind me.

I couldn't wait to start this next chapter,

and this chapter began the following Monday.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c0ST!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe36bd9cd-c1f3-4e90-a605-1ec64666a2e0_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!c0ST!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe36bd9cd-c1f3-4e90-a605-1ec64666a2e0_2848x414.png)My experience in Antler was the most wild rollercoaster ride of my life.

Ten weeks of the highest highs, the lowest lows and everything in between.

I can recount my experiences here, but this experience deserves its own post.

I gained so much from that experience - invaluable knowledge, a strong network, and new friendships.

Most importantly, it confirmed that the entrepreneurial path was designed for me,

and I am designed for it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zJpz!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F09fceb1a-9220-467c-a736-1e2058598f08_2848x414.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zJpz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F09fceb1a-9220-467c-a736-1e2058598f08_2848x414.png)It's been a year since my Antler experience, and it's been a hell of a year.

I am excited to share my journey and insights with you.
