[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)The atmosphere is comprised of

78% Nitrogen,

21% Oxygen,

1% Argon,

and other Gases in minorities (Carbon Dioxide, Water Vapor, Methane).

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)You’re not breathing 79% of the atmosphere.

You’re breathing 21% of the atmosphere.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)From the whole,

you’re extracting 21%.

From the whole,

you’re extracting a part.

From the whole,

you’re extracting what you need.

From the whole,

you’re extracting what you value.

From the whole,

you’re extracting what you need for your survival.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)Similarly,

when you’re confronted with a person,

or an ideology,

or a system,

you don’t need to like 100% of them.

You don’t need to agree with 100% of it.

You don’t need to accept 100% of it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)From the whole,

you should extract 21%.

Extract a part.

Extract what you need.

Extract what you value.

Extract what you need for your survival.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)There are different types of people.

There are different types of ideologies.

There are different types of systems.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)If you need more than 21%,

extract more.

If you need less than 21%,

extract less.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Exkh!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c400c10-9772-4df0-816e-ce971a7a7889_1007x147.png)The rest?

You don’t need it.

You don’t need to hate it.

You don’t need to agree with it.

You don’t need to not like it.

After all,

do you hate 79% of the atmosphere?

or do you simply choose to ignore it?
