It’s 3:25 am on a Thursday,

I went to bed at 11 pm and I’m still up.

I’m struggling to sleep.

I’m thinking about my startup,

permutation after permutation,

iteration after iteration.

I’m dreaming while awake.

Thinking about my first newsletter post also kept me up.

I already have a list of planned content.

I’ve accumulated a lot of unique experiences over the last 6 years.

Venture Building, Growth hacking, Strategy Consulting, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Fundraising, Design, Operations, Branding, No-code building. 

I’ve also built my social capital strategically.

I’ve networked with VCs, Angels, Mentors and Startup Founders,

and I’m eager to share how all my learnings.

..but how do I introduce myself to the world?

Then I thought,

Launching this newsletter is just like doing a product launch.

You are releasing a product out into the world.

Unless you’ve strategically built traction or a waitlist before the launch,

no one is going to care.

Your first version may be trash and embarrassing. That’s okay.

It may not match your exact vision. That’s okay. Be patient.

It may not have all the bells and whistles you think it needs. That’s okay. Start with the feature that solves the most problematic pain and iterate from there. 

..and that’s why I wrote this newsletter at 3:25 am.

I’m not ready, but I’ve started.

I started writing.

I may cringe at this in the future, or even when I wake up tomorrow morning, which is very likely.

I may think of 5-10 different ways to say the same thing, or a more better word to describe what I’m saying

I may have done hours of research to find facts and figures to back my claims.

But the truth is, I know none of that matters.

I will learn how to write a newsletter and I’ll improve, I know it

Just like the gym, it’s all about the reps 

It only matters that you don’t delay and start today.

Some closing thoughts:

What is one thing you’ve been procrastinating on? 

What is one step you can take towards this action?

What’s stopping you from taking that step today?

Let me know your answers!
