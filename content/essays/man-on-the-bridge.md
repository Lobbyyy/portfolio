[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)A man stands on a bridge.

He is thinking about ending it all.

His wife wants a divorce because he is not present,

his kids hate him for being absent,

he's in debt for his past mistakes,

And he lost his job because he lost everything else.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)Like a slow-moving asteroid, these failures had been approaching for years.

Visible on the horizon of his consciousness.

He had looked away, hoping it would somehow change course.

But now the impact was inevitable.

His life lay in ruins, a crater of his own making.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)He feels defeated.

He doesn't want to live anymore.

He feels like he can't go another day like this.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)He looks down.

He can see his toes floating in the air.

The cold chill of the steel on his foot.

The slight crevices of the bolts on the arches of his feet.

The wafers of rust caused by oxidation stuck to his sole.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)He looks beyond his feet.

He sees water.

He sees waves crashing,

undiscriminating.

A pattern of ordered chaos.

Flowing one into another.

Each one connected to the last,

the ripple relaying a secret.

He thinks about all the waves he caused,

and all the ripples that ensued in his life.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)He look up,

and his gaze locks on the horizon.

He’s looked at the sky plenty of times in his life.

Like a gift taken for granted,

it’s always been there.

A dome that has always existed,

a dome that he thinks will always exist.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)For some reason, the sky looks different today.

The sky feels different today.

He realises that he’s looked but he’s not truly seen.

The pink, orange hues of a sunset paint the sky

The colours are in an effortless dance,

flowing,

mixing,

kissing.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!U4P1!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F436fa7fa-0118-4cf8-a108-950bb695bfe5_2846x462.png)He opens his arms as if to give a warm hug.

The type of hug you give someone when you know you might not see them again.

He hears the echoes of birds chirping in the nearby trees.

The gentle wind parcels their song.

They sound different today.

He's heard these songs before, but he hasn't truly listened.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ro0b!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Faf677d8b-ea51-4111-8777-cc4f6b13fd6d_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Ro0b!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Faf677d8b-ea51-4111-8777-cc4f6b13fd6d_2846x462.png)He breathes in.

It’s an inhale with intent.

He feels the air.

He notices his nostrils expanding ever so slightly,

the incremental dilation of the windpipe as the air commutes to his lungs.

But the air doesn't seem to enter his lungs,

This breath submerges him.

He feels it in his bones,

he feels it in his muscles,

he feels it in his nerves,

he feels it in his skin.

The air seeps through his whole body by osmosis,

his whole body feels alive.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!v5hl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9aea95d6-09d5-4a6b-a7d7-e33a8a7b1f89_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!v5hl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9aea95d6-09d5-4a6b-a7d7-e33a8a7b1f89_2846x462.png)At first it's a tingle,

a slight touch,

a caress,

a careful stroke of an artist on a canvas.

But he realises it's more than that.

It's an acknowledgement of his presence in this moment.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He thinks to himself,

is this what presence feels like?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He has lived all his life,

But this is the first breath he has taken.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He exhales.

It's a soft, silent, and sombre exhale.

He realises something,

he's never paid attention to his exhale before.

It's a strange feeling.

He feels light,

he feels vast,

he feels infinity.

Like a floating cloud that observes its own reflection in the still ocean water beneath it,

he sees himself for the first time.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)At this sight,

he feels unburdened.

The weight of his ambition,

the need to compete,

the longing to fit in,

the fight for survival,

he feels those no longer.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)The only thing he feels is peace.

The only thing he feels is presence.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He thinks to himself, 

if he had to do life over again, 

he would do it differently.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He readies himself and lifts his left leg,

It may have taken just a second,

But it felt like an eternity.

The universe could have come in and out of existence a hundred times over in that moment.

Thousands, millions, billions of moments compressed into that one moment.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)His left leg reaches the peak of his stride.

He is about to lean into the waves.

Suddenly,

something catches his eye.

He sees a twinkle in the sky.

It catches him off guard.

It sparks curiosity and wonder.

He retreats his left leg and stares in amazement for a few minutes.

He's still.

Observing intently.

He notices slight glimmers,

as if the light is fading in and out of consciousness,

as if the distant light is alive.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He wasn't looking for a sign to save him,

But if there ever was a sign, he thinks this would be it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He becomes aware of the weight in his pocket.

It was vibrating this whole time,

but he hadn’t noticed it in his stillness.

For months, he had ignored his phone, dreading its demands.

But now, with curious presence, he reaches for it.

The same hand that moments ago was ready to let go,

now moves with gentle intention.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He reads multiple messages from his wife:

"Have you heard the news??"

"??"

"Where are you?"

"There is a massive asteroid heading towards earth and it's going to hit us in 24 hours"

"It's going to wipe out the whole earth, scientists say there is nothing we can do"

"WHERE R U"

"Call me when you get this pls"

"We're at home, where are you???"

"The kids are scared, I'm scared, we need you right now"

His immediate thought isn’t to panic.

He stands still in deep contemplation.

His face glows with warm serenity,

his cheeks and jaw relaxed,

his eyes were fixed on the twinkle in the sky.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)Eventually,

he raises a soft smile.

As if he had arrived at a satisfactory realisation.

He was thinking about ending it all,

ending it on the bridge,

but in the moment closest to death,

he realised what it means to be alive.

He realised he's always had a choice.

He didn't have a choice to be born,

but he has a choice to continue living,

a choice to exist,

a choice to be present,

a choice to let go of everything that's pulling him down,

a choice to grab everything that's lifting him,

a choice to start over,

a choice to care about the things he cares about,

a choice to not care about the things he doesn't care about,

a choice to be grateful for the things he’s taken for granted,

a choice to acknowledge his experiences, but realise he is not only the sum of their parts,

a choice to forgive himself for his past mistakes,

a choice to stay optimistic in the most pessimistic of life situations,

a choice to control the things he has control of,

a choice to let go of the things he has no control over. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0GfP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F30da4d75-330b-4511-ad37-2230968633fd_2846x462.png)He knows the twinkle will bring death to the planet,

But the twinkle also gave him a second chance at life.

Even if it is just for 24 hours,

the man on the bridge knows exactly he is going to spend the rest of his life.
