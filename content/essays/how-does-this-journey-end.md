[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zjqj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F38246502-5060-4dea-abfb-8610d4fd9b56_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zjqj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F38246502-5060-4dea-abfb-8610d4fd9b56_1877x194.png)Do you ever negotiate with yourself?

I used to,

but not anymore.

I used to always give myself a way out.

An exit plan.

If plan A didn’t work, it didn’t matter.

I had plan B.

If plan B didn’t work, it didn’t matter.

I had plan C.

If plan C didn’t work, it didn’t matter.

I had plan D.

If plan D didn’t work, it didn’t matter.

I had plan E.

In my head, this was an exercise in risk mitigation.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yb14!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F428c2525-d679-4a68-9090-15d7ab807d53_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yb14!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F428c2525-d679-4a68-9090-15d7ab807d53_1877x194.png)Thinking through all the exit plans.

It was exhausting creating various different permutations for my life,

and putting the foundations in place to execute those plans.

At one point, I had 6 different versions of my CV.

One tailored for Sales.

One tailored for VC.

One tailored for Finance.

One tailored for Strategy Consulting.

One tailored for Product Management.

One tailored for Founders Associate.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KqBR!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa6c0c3a6-53eb-40df-8766-443d17c433a7_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KqBR!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa6c0c3a6-53eb-40df-8766-443d17c433a7_1877x194.png)[My board member](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/half-my-board-is-dead) taught me to be water, and I am.

I spent the last 7 years up-skilling.

I spent time learning and spent time unlearning.

I learnt how to learn.

I am potential energy.

Waiting to be transformed and shaped into whatever environment I am in.

Seamlessly flowing and fitting into any industry and position.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!h4UU!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F184471f1-5c46-4f22-bca0-49c3ccaa7587_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!h4UU!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F184471f1-5c46-4f22-bca0-49c3ccaa7587_1877x194.png)Earlier this year, I found myself in a strange position.

I could do any of these roles, but I didn’t want to.

I didn’t care about any of these roles.

It’s not what I wanted.

It’s not part of my goals or [my vision](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/the-vision), and it’s not part of my dream.

Therefore, I scrapped all my plans and double downed on entrepreneurship.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!l8ES!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fca251af1-c963-4339-bc78-b92d368f3395_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!l8ES!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fca251af1-c963-4339-bc78-b92d368f3395_1877x194.png)Now, I’ve engineered a simple and [perfect life](https://deckandadream.substack.com/p/engineering-a-perfect-life).

I applied Elon Musk’s five-step process for design thinking and eliminated everything that wasn’t necessary.

In doing so, I reduced the potential permutations of my life to three outcomes.

*Permutation 1:* I die before I ‘make it’.

*Permutation 2:* I go insane ‘making it’.

*Permutation 3:* I ‘make it’.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kUS8!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ad122a2-b75a-4e58-9881-8d72d07fd143_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kUS8!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ad122a2-b75a-4e58-9881-8d72d07fd143_1877x194.png)There are no ifs,

no buts,

no exit options,

no plan B,

no plan C, D, E …

no other possible outcomes.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GagR!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F53c989f5-a217-456f-b839-bdb0ca7884bf_1877x194.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GagR!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F53c989f5-a217-456f-b839-bdb0ca7884bf_1877x194.png)What will the outcome be?

Only time will tell.

Regardless, I will be happy with any one of these permutations.

*Permutation 1:* I might die before I make it, but I will die doing something I believe in.

*Permutation 2:* I might go insane making it, but I will go insane doing something I love.

*Permutation 3:* I make it doing something I love and believe in.
