They exist on the same scale

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)Their similarity?

Both are conventions.

Both are social contracts.

Both are agreements.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)Enacted by social animals,

juried by social animals,

enforced by social animals.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)Their difference?

Rules can be broken,

Laws can be bent.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)If you violate a rule,

you may be scorned at by other social animals,

you may be penalised socially.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)If you violate a law,

you may be scorned at by other social animals,

you may be penalised socially,

you may be penalised legally.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pMV3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F971362d8-e4ae-4898-a34b-6a74d4814aeb_1004x164.png)In this way,

laws are rules with teeth.
