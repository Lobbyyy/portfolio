[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UOFs!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F19ef2f28-c0ee-4987-ae99-66fd14f72fa6_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UOFs!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F19ef2f28-c0ee-4987-ae99-66fd14f72fa6_940x181.png)Rockets are a marvels of human creation,

a vessel that pushes our limits and boundaries,

a vehicle that facilitates space travel.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)However, if you strip away all the rocket science,

a rocket is just a controlled explosion.

Nothing more.

Nothing less.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)Explosions have negative connotations attached to them,

synonyms of destruction and danger come to mind,

but the explosion isn’t the dangerous part.

The dangerous part is trying to launch without enough power. 

Too little energy and you crash back to earth. 

Too much energy without control and you explode on the launchpad.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)The key is channeling the explosion. 

Directing it. 

using it,

harnessing it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)To escape earth’s gravity, rockets need to achieve escape velocity. 

The speed being 11.2 km per second or 40,270 km per hour.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)Similarly, if you're trying to escape the gravity of your current state, 

you'll need to achieve escape velocity. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)This isn't universal. 

Different planets have different escape velocities. 

Different goals require different amounts of explosive energy. 

---

Earth's escape velocity: 11.2 km/second. 

Jupiter's escape velocity: 59.5 km/second. 

Mars' escape velocity: 5.03 km/second. 

---

Starting a new hobby might be your Mars - requiring moderate energy.

Changing careers might be your Earth - requiring significant energy.

Completely reinventing your life might be your Jupiter - requiring massive energy.

The principle remains the same. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EOSH!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F907e4e32-3c87-4ae2-871d-16bddf67f71c_940x181.png)So how do you channel your explosion? 

Know your target trajectory before you ignite. 

Release energy in controlled stages, not all at once. 

Time your launch for maximum impact. 

Don't be afraid to explode. 

Just make sure you're channeling it towards your goals.
