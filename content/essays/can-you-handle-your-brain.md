Can you handle your brain?

It’s almost trivial,

a non-starter question.

But it’s your first time being human,

and it’s the first time you’re in your body.

It’s the first time you are experiencing your brain,

and the first time you’re experiencing its capabilities.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)The brain is the computational engine of your body.

The body is the chassis that holds everything together.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)Like a car engine, you need fuel.

Your body and your brain use this fuel.

You need different types of fuel for each.

You need calories for your body.

You need knowledge for your brain.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)I’ll be the first to admit,

I’ve struggled to handle my brain.

Like a Ferrari engine in a Mini Cooper chassis,

there is an inherent incompatibility.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)Why put a Ferrari engine in a Mini Cooper’s body?

It’s overkill.

A Mini Cooper belongs on the road,

A Ferrari belongs on a race track.

Mixing the two feels creative,

but it belongs nowhere,

and it achieves no purpose other than confusion.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)I’ve struggled to deal with my brain,

maybe it’s all the knowledge I’ve consumed,

the entire set of experiences,

or it may just be my brain.

It’s what keeps me switched on all the time,

it’s what drives my insomnia,

it’s what leads me to form 7 different permutations before you finish your sentence.

And it’s what makes me understand what you are saying, even though you haven’t said a word.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)So, what do you do if you find yourself in the same situation?

You have two options:

Either downgrade your brain to work on the road,

or upgrade your body to work on the race track.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)I’ve tried both.

I’ve used substances to slow my brain to operate on the road, and it’s worked.

I spent a lot of my early twenties like this.

Tranquillising your horsepower,

taming the beast to fit in.

I wouldn’t recommend this.

It’s unsustainable,

and it’s a denial of your true nature.

It’s a denial of your gift.

Those horses want to run free,

to stampede and gallop across meadows.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)You soon realise that you spent all this time trying to fit in,

but you don’t enjoy the road.

You don’t want to sit in a traffic jam.

You don’t want to have fits of road rage,

You don’t want to be stuck in first gear all the time,

you hate the mundaneness of it all.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)The other option?

Upgrade your body to work on the race track.

This is what I’d recommend.

Work on your body,

not because of vanity or the acquisition of abs,

but to be compatible with your brain.

Work on it so you can handle the extreme G forces,

work on it so you can accelerate and decelerate at will,

work on it so you don’t fall apart at high speeds or tight corners.

The goal here isn’t to be a Playboy car,

the goal is to drive compatibility.

To give your brain an appropriate vessel,

so you can discover your true potential.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)How do you work on your body?

First, strengthen the frame itself through demanding physical training.

I've found endurance and strength training works especially well.

Then, strengthen the brain-body interface.

It's the integration system between the brain and the body.

It's the neural pathways that link your engine to your chassis.

Mental practices such as meditation fine-tune this connection,

and emotional work through therapy or journaling helps maintain these systems.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4cAK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0e202c-8b13-48e7-a43f-1952baa5c615_945x162.png)The best thing about this approach:

Once you’ve upgraded your chassis,

you can handle the intensity of the race track when you need it,

and cruise comfortably on regular roads when you don’t.

You’re no longer at war with your own nature,

your brain and body are compatible.

You *are* your nature, *fully expressed*.
