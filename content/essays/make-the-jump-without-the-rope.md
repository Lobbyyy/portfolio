[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yrGo!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fab9192c2-2e3b-4622-be79-2864af439deb_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!yrGo!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fab9192c2-2e3b-4622-be79-2864af439deb_2108x406.png)The Dark Knight has to be one of the best trilogies to exist.

Christopher Nolan did a masterful job,

arguably producing some of the best content from the DCU.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!dML3!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7df14fe7-679c-4116-8aef-c3db04534160_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!dML3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7df14fe7-679c-4116-8aef-c3db04534160_2108x406.png)I cycled over to my friend's place and kept thinking about an iconic scene from the Dark Knight Rises.

In the scene, Bruce Wayne is overpowered in Gotham,

beaten up by Bane,

and banished to "a pit, where men are thrown to suffer and die".

In this pit, he meets others who have also been punished.

These individuals have accepted their fate, but Bruce hasn't.

Bruce needs to escape and get back to save his beloved city.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!x0nw!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F56267836-d179-4a26-8b3c-9f23328ddc3f_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!x0nw!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F56267836-d179-4a26-8b3c-9f23328ddc3f_2108x406.png)This pit is a deep hole in the ground, described by Bane as "the worst hell on Earth".

The pit has an opening at the top,

and there is even a safety rope,

but to reach the top, you need to climb up the walls

and make a death-defying jump across a ledge.

Many people have climbed the walls successfully, but they all falter when they reach the ledge.

Only one person has made the jump.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4vny!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F445e2c5a-22dd-4266-812d-e7920f441a58_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4vny!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F445e2c5a-22dd-4266-812d-e7920f441a58_2108x406.png)Bruce attempts to make the jump twice but fails both times.

His life is only saved by the safety rope that is attached to his waist.

After this second attempt, he gains some wisdom from a blind prisoner.

This wisdom is profound.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!51P5!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd4deb224-7f47-4f32-891e-a88926e5fba9_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!51P5!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd4deb224-7f47-4f32-891e-a88926e5fba9_2108x406.png)***Blind Prisoner:** You do not fear death. You think this makes you strong. It makes you weak.*

***Bruce Wayne:** Why?*

***Blind Prisoner:** How can you move faster than possible, fight longer than possible without the most powerful impulse of the spirit: the fear of death?*

***Bruce Wayne:** I do fear death. I fear dying in here, while my city burns, and there's no one there to save it.*

***Blind Prisoner:** Then make the climb!*

***Bruce Wayne:** How?*

***Blind Prisoner:** As the child did. Without the rope. Then fear will find you again*.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rfvx!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8ae8edee-e38e-4e9b-adc1-296c112fa468_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rfvx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8ae8edee-e38e-4e9b-adc1-296c112fa468_2108x406.png)[Subscribe now](https://deckandadream.substack.com/subscribe?)

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!XesA!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F594ff44e-dafe-40cf-acaf-f8586126e1c8_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!XesA!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F594ff44e-dafe-40cf-acaf-f8586126e1c8_2108x406.png)This conversation is a monumental pivot point of the whole movie.

It highlights the difference between physical strength and spiritual strength.

Bruce has always prioritised the physical and dismissed the spiritual.

In this conversation, he learns that the power of spiritual strength exceeds the physical.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-YNB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe6081eb3-9e59-48c2-ae62-e74530554aa3_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-YNB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe6081eb3-9e59-48c2-ae62-e74530554aa3_2108x406.png)He attempts another climb, this time without the rope - just as the child did.

He gets to the ledge

and jumps.

For what seems like an eternity, he is suspended between life and death.

This time, the fear of death ignites his spirits.

His spirit to live.

His spirit to make the jump.

His spirit to save his city.

It's a powerful impulse.

He makes the jump, clinging on with his fingers.

He survives and returns to Gotham just in time to save it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!z1uw!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9b18837a-1c4b-41e9-aba5-b0d42f303454_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!z1uw!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9b18837a-1c4b-41e9-aba5-b0d42f303454_2108x406.png)When I think about the entrepreneurial journey I've been on this year,

It has also felt like a leap.

A leap with no safety rope.

It's been a weird type of leap, though,

I thought I was only leaping into entrepreneurship.

But indirectly,

I've also taken the leap in other aspects

A leap into self-sufficiency.

A leap into accountability.

A leap into responsibility.

A leap into stewardship.

A leap into loneliness.

A leap into adventure.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!heBq!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52f20678-f4a7-47dc-a995-3df669962705_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!heBq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52f20678-f4a7-47dc-a995-3df669962705_2108x406.png)I can only reflect on this in hindsight.

Honestly, I thought I was only taking a leap into entrepreneurship.

That was what I signed up for.

I wasn't expecting the other stuff,

I didn't realise what was required of me,

I didn't realise how tough it was going to be.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8HiP!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F88ba89f1-b0af-42fd-b6c0-12768699bb4e_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8HiP!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F88ba89f1-b0af-42fd-b6c0-12768699bb4e_2108x406.png)Right now, I'm mid-jump.

Suspended between life and death.

In *[How does this journey end?](https://open.substack.com/pub/deckandadream/p/how-does-this-journey-end?r=r7j0y&amp;utm_campaign=post&amp;utm_medium=web&amp;showWelcomeOnShare=true) *I explored some potential scenarios.

It's pretty simple.

There are only three options.

Two of those options are down scenarios; the only up scenario is that I make it.

I don't have any safety rope.

No exit options.

This is done by design.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ybCl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2868abf1-29b9-4b6f-8f91-405ce3d9dfb1_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ybCl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2868abf1-29b9-4b6f-8f91-405ce3d9dfb1_2108x406.png)One of Sun Tzu's strategies is not giving yourself a way out.

In Art of War, I learnt that when your back is against the wall, you fight like hell.

You fight to survive.

When you give yourself no option to retreat,

you have to survive,

or you perish.

In War, this translates to the notion of "burn the boats and bridges".

Imagine being a soldier in battle in hostile territory.

How would you feel if your General just burnt your only exit?

You would fight like hell.

You must overcome the challenges - whatever they may be.

Psychologically, it activates your fight&nbsp;flight and freeze&nbsp;response.

I imagine that is why Bruce made the jump,

and it's also why I will make it the jump

or die trying.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!aVPL!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F71f5e9f3-c2f3-443e-b6b8-c2eba0900f15_2108x406.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!aVPL!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F71f5e9f3-c2f3-443e-b6b8-c2eba0900f15_2108x406.png)The fear of not making the jump is a powerful impulse.

It's propelling me,

propelling me out of my comfort zone.

It's put me in a different state of mind.

At the start, this feeling was overbearing and overwhelming,

but with time, I have learnt to channel this feeling.

I've learnt to transmute the feeling.

I've seen the beauty in the abyss 

and even acquired a taste for eating glass.

Still,

this journey has been riddled with unforeseen challenges.

You have a plan until you get punched in the face.

These challenges have caused me to freeze,

and sometimes I feel like quitting and taking flight,

but then I remember.

I can't.

Because I burnt the bridge.

Oops.
