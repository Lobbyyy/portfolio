It’s a direction you can move.

In the world we experience, we can move in three directions.

Left or right; forward or backwards; up or down.

But what if there was another direction, 

what if we could move in and out?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)# The 1D world: Life on a line.

Imagine you're a tiny ant. 

You can only walk on a tightrope. 

You can go forward. 

You can go backwards. 

That's it. 

You can't step off the rope. 

There is no "off the rope" in your world.

---

Another ant approaches you from ahead. 

All you see is a dot. 

As it gets closer, the dot gets bigger. 

As it passes and walks away, the dot gets smaller. 

Then it disappears.

You have no idea what the other ant actually looks like. 

You can only see the tiny slice where it touches your line.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)# The 2D World: Life on a Flat Surface

Now imagine you're that ant. 

But you live on a piece of paper. 

You can walk forward. 

You can walk backwards. 

You can walk left. 

You can walk right. 

But you can't fly up. 

You can't dig down. 

There is no "up" or "down" in your world.

---

A ball rolls across your paper. 

What do you see? 

Just circles.

First, a small circle appears. 

Then it gets bigger. 

Then smaller. 

Then it disappears.

You have no clue it was actually a ball. 

You can only see the flat slice where the ball touches your paper.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)# The 3D World: Where We Live

This is our world. 

We can move forward or backward. 

We can move left or right. 

We can move up or down. 

We can see the full shape of things. 

The whole ball. 

The whole person. 

The whole tree.

We experience the world and see three-dimensional representations of objects.

But what happens if we expand our capabilities to the fourth dimension? 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)# The 4D world: our next evolution

In the fourth dimension,

our three dimensions still exist, 

but they become just one slice of a much larger reality.

Just like the paper-ant only saw circular slices of the ball in 2D space.

---

In the fourth dimension, you don't just see the surface. 

You see through it. 

All of it. 

At once.

Think about a cube. 

When you look at it, you see the outside faces. 

But a 4D being? 

They see inside the cube. 

They see all six faces simultaneously. 

They see the interior space. 

They see everything.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)Going from a 3D being to a 4D being is a two-step process.

Step 1: See through matter.

Step 2: See through time.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)## Step 1: See through matter

Look at a person walking down the street. 

Most people see skin. 

Hair. 

Clothes. 

The surface.

However, this is only a slice of the full picture.

---

A 4D being sees deeper. 

They see the skin that wraps the body,

but it doesn’t stop there.

The 4D being sees all the layers. 

The 4D being sees everything.

---

They see the epidermis, the outermost layer.

Dead cells are constantly shedding.

A protective barrier against the world.

---

They see the dermis beneath. 

Living tissue. 

Blood vessels branching like rivers. 

Nerve endings are sending signals. 

Hair follicles are anchored deep. 

Sweat glands cool the system.

---

They see the subcutaneous tissue. 

The fat layer underneath. 

Insulation. 

Energy storage. 

Cushioning for impact.

---

They see the fascia. 

The thin wrapping around everything. 

Connecting tissue to tissue. 

Muscle to bone. 

A web holding the body together.

---

They see the muscles. 

Hundreds of them. 

Contracting and releasing. 

Pulling bones. 

Creating movement. 

Each fibre is threaded and woven. 

Generating heat. 

Burning fuel.

---

They see deeper still.

---

The tendons. 

Tough cords connecting muscle to bone. 

Transferring force. 

Making movement possible.

---

They see the ligaments. 

Bone to bone connections. 

Stabilising joints. 

Preventing dislocation. 

Keeping everything in place.

---

They see the bones themselves. 

206 of them. 

Calcium and phosphorus. 

Marrow inside produces blood cells. 

The scaffolding of the body.

---

They see the nervous system. 

Electrical highways. 

Signals racing at 300 mph. 

Brain to finger. 

Eye to processing centre. 

Thought to action.

The body's internet.

---

They see the circulatory system. 

60,000 miles of blood vessels. 

Heart pumping. 

Oxygen delivery. 

Waste removal. 

Nutrients transported. 

The body's supply chain.

---

They see the lymphatic system. 

The cleanup crew. 

Fighting infection. 

Draining fluid. 

Maintaining immunity.

---

They see the organs. 

The heart beating 100,000 times a day.

Lungs exchanging gases. 

Kidneys filtering blood. 

Liver processing toxins. 

Each organ is a factory. 

Each with a specialised job.

---

They see it all. 

Simultaneously. 

Layer upon layer. 

System upon system. 

All transparent. 

All visible. 

All functioning.

---

This person isn't just walking. 

They're a symphony of biological systems. 

An orchestra.

All coordinated. 

All working together in harmony.

As a fourth dimensional being, you see it all.

All at once.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)## Step 2: See through time.

After you’ve conquered physical depth perception,

you’re ready to progress to time depth perception.

---

Take an old man sitting on a bench.

3D beings just see the old man,

but a 4D being sees all versions of the old man.

The slices of the old man’s past,

the current slice being observed,

and all the slices of the old man’s future. 

They see all the layers.

They see the baby he was. 

The child learning to walk.

The teenager falling in love. 

The young man starting his career. 

The father holding his newborn. 

The grandfather teaching his grandson to fish.

All existing simultaneously. 

All visible. 

All transparent.

All at once.

---

Another example:

Hold a seed in the palm of your hand. 

3D beings see a tiny seed.

A 4D being sees the mighty oak. 

The sapling breaking through soil. 

The decades of growth. 

The storms it will weather. 

The seasons of change it experiences.

The shade it will provide.

The birds that will nest in its branches. 

The eventual decay. 

The nutrients returning to earth. 

The cycle completing. 

All visible. 

All happening now.

All at once.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)Once you look beyond the surface

and you see everything,

and you see every time,

you’ll become a fourth dimensional being. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GCTl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe25095d2-c506-4b28-be6b-d62630faefe6_943x154.png)You may be asking: why be fourth-dimensional beings?

My answer: Why *not* be fourth-dimensional beings?

---

Just because we’ve always done something one way,

does that mean we should continue doing it?

---

Just because we’ve always seen through three dimensions,

does that mean we should continue doing it?

---

Humanity keeps optimising the same variables. 

Faster times. 

Higher jumps. 

Bigger buildings. 

Smarter algorithms.

---

We measure progress in deltas.

Take long distance running for example:

Marathon world record in 1920: 2:32:35. 

Marathon world record today: 2:00:35. 

Delta: 32 minutes faster.

The delta in three dimensions is always finite.

---

And we've been playing the same game for 300,000 years.

Still competing in three dimensions. 

Still seeing surface-level improvements.

Still repeating the same mistakes.

---

What if the next breakthrough isn't about going faster? 

What if it's about seeing deeper? 

What if true evolution isn't about optimising what we can already see, 

but about seeing what we've been missing entirely?

---

Fourth-dimensional thinking isn't just an upgrade. 

It's a completely different operating system.

The delta between 3D and 4D beings is infinite.

---

We've been conditioned to accept surface-level perception.

We've been told that seeing deeper isn't possible.

That we're limited to what's directly in front of us.

That thinking beyond the obvious is just fantasy.

---

But what if that's just conditioning?

What if we've been operating with training wheels on?

---

Look at what's already happening around us.

LLMs process information in hundreds, thousands and even millions of dimensions,

seeing patterns and connections we can’t even compute.

They're proof that higher-dimensional computation isn't impossible.

Just untrained.
