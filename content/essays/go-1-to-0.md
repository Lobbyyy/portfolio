[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)The phrase 0 to 1 is often referenced in relation to startups.

It was made popular by Venture Capitalist Peter Thiel.

His book is called Zero to One,

and it is a must read for any aspiring entrepreneur.

I’ve read it multiple times.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)Going from zero to one is a process.

Its when you go from nothing,

being at 0,

to something,

to being 1.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)What you may not realise is starting at 0 is a blessing

Not everyone starts at 0,

Sometimes,

you start at -1,

when there is less than nothing.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)How can there be less than nothing?

First you need to understand the state of being at 0.

It’s the state where your product or idea doesn’t exist,

it may exist in your mind,

but it doesn’t exist in the physical domain.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)To be in a -1 state is to be less than nothing.

It’s the state where the product doesn’t exist,

and your belief in your ability to transform the product or idea from 0 to 1 also doesn’t exist.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)It’s the state of doubt,

it’s the state of disbelief.

It’s the..

“It would have never worked anyway”

“If only I had X, I could do it”

“If only I was Y, I could do it”

“I would need X, Y, and Z to do it”

“I know person X did it, but they were Y so that’s why they succeeded”

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)You can substitute any variables you want for X, Y and Z.

Time,

Money,

Connections,

Ideas,

Skills,

Traits,

Luck.

Whatever you want.

Throw the english dictionary at it.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)Sometimes,

before you go from 0 to 1,

you need to go from -1 to 0.

You need to build the belief,

the confidence,

the self-esteem,

to begin the journey from 0.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)It’s the climb before the climb.

It’s the story before the story.

It’s the origin before the origin.

It’s where the end of a marathon,

is just the starting line of the another.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)It’s not a disadvantage starting at -1.

I can confirm this with confidence,

because it is where I started.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)The journey to the starting line,

is a journey in itself.

The things you see,

the experiences you have,

the trials and tribulations you face.

They will strengthen your resolve.

It is the fuel you need for your journey to 0 to 1.

Without it, you may run out of gas between 0 to 1.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)The journey from 0 to 1 is fraught with challenges.

Some of them small,

some Goliath.

You may overcome some,

but others may feel unsurmountable.

In that moment when you feel you can’t get to 1,

what will steady your spirit?

what will give you the ability to see through the storm?

what will ground your feet?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)Before you begin your journey, assess your starting point.

Ask yourself,

where are you starting from?

are you really starting from 0?

do you have the belief that you can make it to 1?

Be honest with yourself.

Don’t lie to yourself.

Don’t ever lie to yourself.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)Some questions to help you identify your starting point:

Do you really have the belief that you can do it?

How do you know?

What set of evidence do you have?

How do you know you will stick with it when shit hits the fan?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)If you’re starting at 0, then that’s great.

Begin that journey and I wish you good fortune on your path.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!_O5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2a43c48c-1ea2-41d7-916c-6c36deb8e97d_1008x176.png)If you’re not starting at 0, then that’s great.

Begin your journey to the starting line and I wish you good fortune on your path.

By the time you reach the starting line,

you will be prepared for the journey ahead,

because you know you’ve already completed the climb before the climb.
