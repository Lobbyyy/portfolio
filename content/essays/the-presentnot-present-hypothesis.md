CORE THESIS**

Reality operates not as a sequence of causal events flowing through time, but as a system of simultaneous mutual constraints between two fundamental time-states: Present (P) and Not-Present (~P). What we experience as “choice” and “determinism” are not contradictory forces but complementary aspects of the same constraint relationship, analogous to yin-yang complementarity and quantum entanglement.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t1un!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89c1e213-cafb-4837-8b35-d0df2eefebaa_2124x282.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!t1un!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F89c1e213-cafb-4837-8b35-d0df2eefebaa_2124x282.png)### **II. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES**

**A. Binary Time Ontology**

Traditional thinking divides time into three states: past, present, and future. This hypothesis reduces time to its logical first principles:

**Present (P)**: The state where observation and action occur

**Not-Present (~P)**: Everything that is not Present, encompassing both what we conventionally call “past” and “future”

**Rationale:** From a logical standpoint, there are only two fundamental states: “now” and “not-now.” The distinction between past and future is derivative, not fundamental.

**B. Action-Observation Framework**

Within the Present (P), two fundamental actions are possible regarding quantum systems:

**Action a**: Observation/measurement occurs

**Action b**: No observation/measurement occurs

These actions at P determine states at ~P:

**State x**: Particle behaviour

**State y**: Wave behaviour

**C. Constraint Relationships**

The core mathematical structure:

If a (observe) at P → then x (particle) at ~P 

If b (don’t observe) at P → then y (wave) at ~P

These are not causal relationships but *simultaneous mutual constraints*. P and ~P don’t exist in temporal sequence. They exist as complementary aspects of one unified system that must satisfy internal consistency requirements.

EVIDENCE FROM QUANTUM MECHANICS**

**A. Wave-Particle Duality**

 In the double-slit experiment: 

- When we observe which slit a photon passes through → particle behaviour 

- When we don’t observe → wave interference pattern

**B. Delayed-Choice Experiments**

Wheeler’s delayed-choice experiment demonstrates that decisions made at time t₂ determine the quantum state at earlier time t₁. In the Present/Not-Present framework, this isn’t “backwards causation” but rather evidence that P and ~P are mutually constraining.

**C. Quantum Entanglement**

Entangled particles exhibit correlated states regardless of spatial separation. This hypothesis suggests temporal “entanglement”, P and ~P are correlated across time the way entangled particles are correlated across space.

### **IV. PHILOSOPHICAL IMPLICATIONS**

**A. Resolving the Free Will vs. Determinism Paradox**

In the Matrix, the Oracle’s offers a sweet to Neo and states: “You’ve already made the choice, now you have to understand it.” 

In the Present/Not-Present framework: 

- The choice is real (you genuinely choose at P) 

- The outcome is determined (the system must satisfy consistency with ~P) 

- These are not contradictory because they describe the same event from different perspectives

Like a completed Sudoku puzzle: Did you fill in the numbers sequentially, or were the solutions always valid across the entire grid simultaneously? Both perspectives are true.

**B. Reinterpreting Causality**

Traditional view: 

Past → Present → Future (sequential causation) 

Present/Not-Present view: 

 P ⊕ ~P = Unified Reality (simultaneous constraint satisfaction) 

Causation is not fundamental. What we perceive as cause-and-effect is the manifestation of consistency requirements between P and ~P.

**C. The Nature of Choice**

Choice exists at P not as freedom from determinism, but as the *necessary act of constraint satisfaction*. Your choice at P is: 

1. Genuinely yours (experienced at P) 

2. Constrained by consistency with ~P 

3. Simultaneously determining and determined 

This is the yin-yang principle: opposites that define each other, neither existing independently.

MATHEMATICAL STRUCTURE**

**A. Constraint Equations**

The system can be represented as: 

State(P) ⊗ State(~P) = Consistent Configuration 

Where ⊗ represents mutual constraint operation

More specifically: 

Choice(a or b) at P ⊗ Outcome(x or y) at ~P = Reality 

Valid pairs: (a,x) or (b,y)

**B. Complementarity Principle**

Like quantum complementary observables (position/momentum), P and ~P are:

Mutually exclusive (you cannot experience both simultaneously)

Mutually defining (neither exists without the other)

Jointly complete (together they constitute reality)

**C. Timeless Constraint Satisfaction**

The equations are satisfied “all at once” in a timeless sense, but we experience them sequentially because we are embedded within the system, always at P.

**E. Entropy and Complementary Time Arrows**

The arrow of time, rooted in entropy’s increase, poses no contradiction in the P/~P framework. From first principles, entropy quantifies configurational possibilities, with the forward arrow emerging as our embedded experience at P: Systems evolve from low-entropy states (constrained, ordered) to high (expansive, disordered), reflecting the cosmic gradient from Big Bang to heat death. This is not fundamental causation but the manifestation of mutual constraints, ~P’s low-entropy boundary defines P’s flow, much like a river’s direction feels absolute from a boat but arises from topography.

The backward arrow, while not directly observable at P, exists as complementary constraints in ~P, ensuring timeless consistency. Quantum systems demonstrate this duality: Forward entropy growth coexists with backward correlations, as in open-system dynamics. Black holes exemplify this: At P, they appear as forward “hoovers,” accreting matter and increasing total entropy via horizon expansion. Yet, the holographic principle reveals backward constraints: In-falling information is encoded on the horizon’s surface as timeless “memory,” preserving quantum consistency without loss. This duality, forward appearance at P, backward revelation in ~P mirrors the framework’s complementarity, unifying entropy’s arrow with timeless reality.

CONCLUSION**

The Present/Not-Present Hypothesis proposes that reality is best understood not as events flowing through time, but as a timeless system of mutual constraints between complementary time-states. What we experience as choice at the Present is simultaneously the determined outcome required for consistency with the Not-Present.

Like the two sides of a coin, the yin and yang, or entangled quantum particles, Present and Not-Present define each other. They are not separate entities with causal relationships, but aspects of one unified reality that must satisfy internal coherence.

This framework suggests that the Matrix’s Oracle was correct: You’ve already made the choice. But so was Neo’s experience of choosing. Both are true because they describe the same reality from the only two perspectives available: being at P, and the constraint relationship with ~P.

The question isn’t whether we have free will OR whether everything is determined. The question is: can we understand that these are two words for the same thing?
