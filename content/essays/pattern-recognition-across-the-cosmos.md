I wrote about the [goldilocks of tim](https://open.substack.com/pub/deckandadream/p/goldilocks-of-time?r=r7j0y&amp;utm_campaign=post&amp;utm_medium=web)e yesterday.

While it’s elegant, I felt it warrants further explanation.

This is the explanation. 

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)I was staring at our Sun the other day,

thinking about death.

Not my death.

The Sun's death.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)Right now, deep in the Sun's core, there's a war happening.

Nuclear fusion is pushing outward, and gravity is pulling inward.

For now, fusion is winning.

The Sun is stable, burning bright, keeping us alive.

But gravity is patient.

Gravity always wins in the end.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)One day, billions of years from now, the fuel will run low.

Fusion will weaken. Gravity will overcome.

The Sun will collapse, then explode, then collapse again.

Death by its own weight.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)As above, so below.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)I believe the universe operates similarly.

The Big Bang was our nuclear ignition,

a moment of all creation and expansion,

The force is so great that it outpaces its own gravity,

for now.

But what if the universe is just like our Sun?

What if expansion is our fusion, and dark matter is our gravity?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)What if the universe is also fighting a war it cannot win?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)Currently, we are in a stable phase.

The main sequence of cosmic time.

We live during the universe's prime,

the Goldilocks zone of time,

tethered between past and future.

Just as life on Earth exists in the Sun's prime,

and in its habitable zone, where temperatures are just right for water to exist.

But equilibrium is temporary.

Everything that expands must contract.

Everything that burns must cool.

Everything that lives must die.

We haven’t reached equilibrium yet,

the universe is still expanding

so we can ignore gravity’s calling,

but like the end of a good book,

you know, there is a last page that gets turned.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)The universe will reach its own moment when expansion weakens,

when gravity calls everything home.

The Big Crunch.

Death by its own weight. And then?

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)Maybe we start over.

Maybe the implosion becomes an explosion.

Maybe death becomes birth.

Maybe yesterday's Big Bang was just the universe's way of saying it's time to go again.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)*"Today I live"* is the only line in the present tense.

The only moment that matters.

Suspended between cosmic birth and cosmic death,

between the Sun's ignition and the Sun's collapse,

between the Big Bang yesterday and the Big Crunch tomorrow.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!64zK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7edbb41f-9803-4ae1-90a2-de4fae1f3b78_900x145.png)We are the universe's fleeting consciousness,

its brief moment of self-awareness,

caught in the middle of its own lifecycle.

Burning bright before the inevitable darkness.

And then we go again.
