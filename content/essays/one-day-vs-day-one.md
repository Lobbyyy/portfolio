[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!e1RI!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5d2e3273-de01-46c1-b9d3-ad47f5185a3c_1167x175.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!e1RI!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F5d2e3273-de01-46c1-b9d3-ad47f5185a3c_1167x175.png)You were something,

You die,

Now you’re something else.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Cg2y!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd1406450-6f99-48e3-bb08-4a474d9e1d04_1167x175.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Cg2y!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd1406450-6f99-48e3-bb08-4a474d9e1d04_1167x175.png)I know I will die one day,

I know everyone I love will die one day,

I know everyone I know will die one day,

I know everyone on earth will die one day,

I know the earth will die one day,

I know the sun will die one day,

I know the galaxy will die one day,

I know the universe will die one day.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ywz0!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F803012cc-dbcf-466d-8198-24c9bc133bee_1167x175.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ywz0!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F803012cc-dbcf-466d-8198-24c9bc133bee_1167x175.png)One day, nothing will matter.

One day, there will be no matter.

No-thing will matter.

But,

Today is not that day.

So,

Today, I will live like it's day one.

And live every single day like it's day one.

Because today every-thing matters,

And one day it might be one day.
