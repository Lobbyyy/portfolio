It’s a feature, not a bug.

I’m not scared of the abyss,&nbsp;

I stand before it,

stare into its vastness,

appreciate its beauty,

and realise it's always existed,

It is only now that I choose to look.

Only now that I have decided not to be ignorant.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lH5e!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fab22d5df-ca29-4ee9-8b28-45316ce2804c_2058x364.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lH5e!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fab22d5df-ca29-4ee9-8b28-45316ce2804c_2058x364.png)People fear what they don’t understand.

That is all I can do.&nbsp;
