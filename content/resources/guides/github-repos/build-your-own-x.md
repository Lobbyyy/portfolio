## What it is

[Build Your Own X](https://github.com/codecrafters-io/build-your-own-x) is the most-starred repository on GitHub, with over 565,000 stars. There's no program to install. It's a list of well-written guides for rebuilding well-known technology from scratch, in many languages.

> "What I cannot create, I do not understand." Richard Feynman

## What you get

30 sections, each with several guides. A few of them:

- **Neural network and AI model**
- **Game, 3D renderer and physics engine**
- **Web browser and web server**
- **Git, Docker and a database**
- **Blockchain / cryptocurrency**
- **Operating system, shell and programming language**

## How to use it

1. Open the repo and pick a section.
2. Choose a guide in a language you know (or one you want to learn).
3. Build along, one step at a time.

Or give the link to your agent and ask it to pick a guide for your level and coach you through it.

## Before you use it

It's free and in the public domain (CC0). The guides are written by many different people, so quality and length vary. Read the first page or two before you commit to one.
