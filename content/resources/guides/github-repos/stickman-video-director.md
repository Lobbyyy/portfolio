## What it is

[Stickman Video Director](https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director) is a skill for Claude Code, Codex and other agents. It turns a topic, an article or a full script into an animated stickman video. It's open source (MIT) with about 8,500 stars on GitHub.

## What you get

- **The whole package.** Script, storyboard, voiceover, captions, sound and a thumbnail.
- **Lots of styles.** Classic black and white, retro TV, cave paintings, noir, pixel theatre and more.
- **Two engines.**
  - **Model Engine (A)** writes a prompt for each scene for a video model. It looks richer, but it costs video credits.
  - **Code Engine (B)** draws reusable characters and animates them in code. It renders on your computer with no video credits.
- **Landscape or portrait.** 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts and TikTok.

## How to set it up

1. Send this to your agent: "Help me install this skill: https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director. After installation, read SKILL.md and tell me you're ready."
2. Pick a style from the README.
3. Ask for a 30-second video first. Approve the outline and storyboard, then let it make the rest.

## Before you use it

The skill is free. The services it connects to are not always free: your agent plan, image or voice tools, and video credits if you use Model Engine (A).
